# Zomato–Blinkit: outside-in synergy and integration brief

## Transaction context
Zomato's 2022 acquisition Q&A described quick commerce as strategically complementary to food delivery. Management said the plan after closing was to experiment with cross-leveraging each platform's customer base and integrating delivery-fleet back ends, while keeping Blinkit as an independent app/brand.

## Observed segment trajectory
| Blinkit quick-commerce metric | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Adjusted revenue (INR crore) | 806 | 2,301 | 5,206 | 37,779* |
| Adjusted EBITDA (INR crore) | (562) | (384) | (292) | (277) |
| NOV (INR crore) | — | — | — | 48,567 |
| Adjusted EBITDA margin (% of NOV) | — | — | — | (0.6%) |

*FY26 adjusted revenue is not comparable with prior years: the quick-commerce business shifted toward inventory ownership, so reported revenue includes the full monetary value of goods sold rather than only marketplace commissions. The company reports 144% FY26 year-on-year like-for-like gross-profit growth, adjusted EBITDA break-even in Q3FY26 and a 0.3% adjusted EBITDA margin as a share of NOV in Q4FY26. It attributes improvement to gross-margin expansion, cost efficiencies and operating leverage. These are Blinkit business-performance indicators; they do not isolate synergies caused by Zomato integration.

FY23 group summary figures reflect consolidation only from 10 August 2022. Some FY23 quick-commerce operating data is full-year unaudited MIS supplied by Blinkit. Do not compare the FY26 reported adjusted revenue directly with FY25.

## Synergy hypotheses
| Lever | Mechanism | Measure | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer cross-leverage | Cross-promote food and grocery use cases to relevant customer cohorts | Incremental active customers, cross-order rate, CAC, incremental contribution | Explicit management plan to experiment; no quantified synergy target identified |
| Delivery operations | Shared delivery-partner/fleet back-end and dispatch capabilities | Cost per delivery, utilisation, batching, idle time, service level | Management-stated plan; require before/after operating metrics |
| Technology | Reuse platform capabilities and technology integrations | Engineering run cost, release cycle, uptime, conversion | Management-stated rationale; not a disclosed saving |
| Merchant/brand ecosystem | Potential cross-platform merchant and brand access | Incremental merchant sales and contribution after incentives | Hypothesis; validate with actual merchant cohort data |

## Risks and confounders
- Quick commerce and restaurant delivery have different order economics, basket sizes, picking/packing costs and service-level expectations.
- Cross-selling may cause discounting or cannibalise existing customer orders rather than create incremental contribution.
- Dark-store density, assortment, shrinkage, inventory turns and fulfilment costs can overwhelm delivery synergies.
- The FY26 revenue accounting change makes the adjusted revenue series non-comparable without an explicit like-for-like bridge.
- The acquisition Q&A framed customer cross-leverage as experimentation, not a guaranteed outcome.

## Management vs estimate vs delivery
- Management stated plan: experiment with customer-base cross-leverage and integrate delivery-fleet back ends; keep Blinkit app independent.
- Reported acquired-business outcomes: FY26 NOV of INR 48,567 crore, adjusted EBITDA loss of INR 277 crore and margin of -0.6% of NOV; break-even achieved in Q3FY26.
- Quantified synergy target / realised amount: not identified in the reviewed sources.
- Independent estimate: still requires cohort-level cross-orders, cost-per-delivery, contribution-margin and control-group evidence.
