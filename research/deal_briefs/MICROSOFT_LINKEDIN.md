# Microsoft–LinkedIn: outside-in synergy and integration brief

## Transaction context
Microsoft completed the acquisition on 8 December 2016 for a total purchase price of USD 27.0bn. Its FY2017 annual report said the acquisition was expected to accelerate growth of LinkedIn, Office 365 and Dynamics 365. LinkedIn contributed approximately USD 2.3bn revenue for the post-acquisition period included in FY2017 results.

## Latest reported LinkedIn revenue trajectory
| Fiscal year | LinkedIn revenue (USD million) | YoY change |
|---|---:|---:|
| FY2023 | 14,989 | — |
| FY2024 | 16,372 | 9.2% |
| FY2025 | 17,812 | 8.8% |
| FY2026 | 19,817 | 11.3% |

Microsoft's FY2026 annual report reports LinkedIn revenue of USD 19.817bn, up 11%. This is an acquired-business scale and growth indicator, not revenue caused by bundling LinkedIn with Office or Dynamics. Microsoft's FY26 reporting also shows Dynamics 365 revenue growth of 18% and Microsoft 365 Commercial cloud revenue growth of 17%; the co-movement of product lines does not establish cross-sell causality.

## Synergy hypotheses
| Lever | Mechanism | Measure | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product bundling | Integrate LinkedIn identity/network and data into Office/Dynamics workflows | Incremental attach, paid seats, ARPU and retention vs control cohort | Management strategic rationale; requires causal attribution |
| Enterprise distribution | Microsoft enterprise relationships distribute LinkedIn Talent/Sales solutions | Incremental enterprise accounts and contribution margin | Hypothesis; reported segment growth alone insufficient |
| Advertising/data | Professional audience and intent signals improve advertising relevance | Incremental yield, advertiser retention and compliance cost | Potential revenue lever; privacy and trust constraints |
| Cost | Shared corporate services and infrastructure | Duplicated cost pool, realised savings net of integration costs | No quantified synergy target identified in reviewed annual reports |

## Observed financial evidence and caveats
- FY2017: LinkedIn contributed approximately USD 2.3bn for the partial period after acquisition.
- FY2018: LinkedIn revenue was USD 5.3bn for a full year; FY2017 was only a partial post-close period. This is not a like-for-like organic growth rate.
- FY2026: LinkedIn revenue was USD 19.817bn, up 11% year over year.
- Segment revenue growth does not identify product attach, incremental contribution or causality.
- Microsoft Cloud includes the commercial portion of LinkedIn; avoid adding this contribution again when reconciling segment totals.

## Management vs estimate vs delivery
- Management rationale: accelerate growth of LinkedIn, Office 365 and Dynamics 365.
- Quantified management synergy target: not identified in the reviewed annual reports.
- Reported acquired-business outcome: LinkedIn revenue of USD 19.817bn in FY26.
- Independent estimate: requires product-level attach, renewal, seat expansion and cohort counterfactuals; standalone revenue is not a substitute.
