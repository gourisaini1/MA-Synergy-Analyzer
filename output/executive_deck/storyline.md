# Five-slide executive deck: evidence-backed storyline

1. **Four-deal executive view:** HDFC is a partial outside-in model; Air India and Zomato have public financial and integration evidence but no attributable synergy estimate; Kraft Heinz has a target, ex-post sensitivity and reported cumulative savings.
2. **HDFC–HDFC Bank:** quantify funding substitution, CRR/SLR opportunity-cost drag and limited operating-cost overlap; show PSL as unverified.
3. **Air India–Vistara:** show pre-merger FY24 entity revenue, merged FY25 revenue/loss and the vendor/system/customer integration milestones; explain why these do not prove financial savings.
4. **Zomato–Blinkit:** show consolidated cost pools and Blinkit segment trajectory; explain why consolidated spend and EBITDA improvement are not standalone integration synergies.
5. **Kraft Heinz:** compare $1.5bn annual run-rate target, $1.40bn ex-post model base, >$1.7bn cumulative reported savings and reported integration-program expense; distinguish annual run-rate from cumulative savings.

The deck does not add unlike currencies or sum target, estimate and realised measures. It makes data gaps and attribution limits explicit.
