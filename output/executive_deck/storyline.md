# Five-slide executive deck: evidence-backed storyline

1. **Executive framing:** distinguish strategic rationale, integration progress, acquired-business performance and attributable merger value.
2. **Deal-specific value tests:** HDFC mortgage/deposit cross-sell; Air India procurement/network; Blinkit cross-orders/delivery efficiency; LinkedIn enterprise product attach.
3. **Observable evidence:** HDFC >90% reported home-loan-to-savings attach; Air India 4,000+ vendor contracts and 140+ systems aligned; Blinkit FY24-FY25 revenue/EBITDA trajectory; LinkedIn FY23-FY25 revenue trend.
4. **Model discipline:** cost and revenue synergy formulas, missing inputs, counterfactual requirements and integration costs.
5. **Diligence priorities:** specific data requests and causal tests required before claiming attributable synergy value.

The deck deliberately does not report a total synergy number because the reviewed public sources do not provide sufficiently granular cost pools, cohort economics or comparable realised synergy disclosures.
